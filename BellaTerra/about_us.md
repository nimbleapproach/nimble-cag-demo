Welcome to Ristorante del Cuore: Where Italy's Soul Meets Your Plate

Nestled in the heart of Kingston upon Hull, Ristorante del Cuore (Restaurant of the Heart) is more than just an Italian restaurant; it is a culinary journey through the diverse and soulful regions of Italy. Our story began with Chef Alessandro Rossi, a third-generation restaurateur from Florence, whose passion for authentic Italian cuisine was ignited in his grandmother's bustling Tuscan kitchen. After years honing his craft in Michelin-starred establishments across Italy and Europe, Alessandro brought his dream to Hull: to create a dining experience that truly reflects the warmth, generosity, and unparalleled flavours of his homeland.

At Ristorante del Cuore, we believe that food is an expression of love, a celebration of life, and a gathering point for family and friends. Our philosophy is rooted in cucina povera – "peasant cooking" – which champions simplicity, exceptional ingredients, and the ingenuity to transform humble elements into extraordinary dishes. This reverence for tradition is woven into every aspect of our menu, from our hand-pulled pasta to our slow-braised meats and our vibrant, seasonal vegetable creations.

Our expansive, yet intimate, dining space accommodates 150 covers, designed to evoke the ambiance of a classic Italian trattoria, complete with rustic stone, warm lighting, and the inviting aromas of fresh herbs and wood-fired ovens. Whether you're seeking a romantic dinner for two, a lively family gathering, or a sophisticated corporate event, Ristorante del Cuore offers an experience that transports you directly to the heart of Italy.

We are committed to:

Authenticity: Staying true to time-honoured Italian recipes and cooking techniques.

Quality: Sourcing the finest ingredients, both locally and directly from Italy, ensuring freshness and provenance.

Passion: Infusing every dish with the dedication and love that defines Italian culinary heritage.

Experience: Providing a memorable dining journey that extends beyond the plate, through impeccable service and a genuine Italian welcome.

Join us at Ristorante del Cuore, and allow us to share the passion, tradition, and heart that define true Italian dining. Benvenuti!
