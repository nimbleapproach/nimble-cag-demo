The Heart of Our Meats: Our Commitment to Exceptional Beef Sourcing

At Ristorante del Cuore, our dedication to authentic Italian cuisine extends deeply into the provenance of every ingredient, especially our meats. For our beef, we adhere to a philosophy centred on quality, ethical sourcing, and flavour integrity, ensuring that every cut served at your table is nothing short of exceptional.

We meticulously select our beef from a network of small, independent farms, primarily nestled in the rolling hills of Tuscany and Piedmont in Italy, and select high-welfare local British farms that mirror these exacting standards. These regions are renowned for their traditional farming methods, which prioritize animal welfare and environmental sustainability. Our chosen suppliers uphold a rigorous approach to cattle rearing:

Mixed Grazing & Natural Diet: Our cattle are primarily grass-fed, roaming freely on expansive pastures, supplemented with a carefully balanced, locally sourced grain diet during specific periods. This balanced approach contributes to a nuanced flavour profile and superior marbling, setting our beef apart.

Ethical Husbandry: We only work with farms that demonstrate an unwavering commitment to the well-being of their animals. This means ample space, natural living conditions, and humane handling practices throughout their lives. We believe that happy, well-cared-for animals yield the finest quality meat.

Ageing Process: Once processed, our beef undergoes a precise dry-ageing process for a minimum of 28 days. This crucial step naturally tenderizes the meat and concentrates its rich, savoury flavours, developing complex notes that are unattainable through conventional methods.

Traceability: We maintain full traceability from farm to fork, allowing us to guarantee the origin and journey of every cut. This transparency is key to our promise of quality and trust.

This uncompromising approach to beef sourcing is evident in every bite, whether you're savouring our iconic Bistecca alla Fiorentina, a robust Osso Buco, or our delicately sliced Tagliata di Manzo. Our beef dishes are not just meals; they are a celebration of tradition, quality, and the passionate pursuit of flavour, reflecting the true essence of Italian culinary heritage.
