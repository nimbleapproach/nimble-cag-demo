The Art of Free-Range: Our Poultry Sourcing Philosophy

At Ristorante del Cuore, even our poultry, often a humble ingredient, is elevated through a philosophy centered on ethical sourcing, exceptional quality, and the pursuit of natural flavour. We believe that well-cared-for birds yield meat that is not only superior in taste and texture but also aligns with our values of responsible animal husbandry.

Our Unwavering Principles for Poultry:

Free-Range and High Welfare: We exclusively source free-range poultry, prioritizing birds that have ample space to roam, forage naturally, and express their natural behaviours. This commitment extends to ensuring they are raised in clean, comfortable environments with access to fresh air and natural light.

Natural Diet: Our chickens are fed a balanced, natural diet, free from growth hormones and unnecessary antibiotics. This contributes directly to the development of lean, flavourful meat with a firm yet tender texture.

Local Partnerships: We build strong, transparent relationships with local British farms that uphold these exacting welfare standards. This not only ensures the freshness of our poultry but also supports our local agricultural community and reduces our carbon footprint.

Provenance and Traceability: We maintain full traceability for all our poultry, from the farm to our kitchen. This allows us to guarantee the origin and conditions under which our birds were raised, reinforcing our commitment to quality and transparency.

Culinary Excellence Through Conscious Sourcing:

This meticulous sourcing allows our chefs to bring out the very best in our poultry dishes. Whether it's the rustic simplicity of our Pollo alla Diavola (a butterflied chicken grilled to spicy perfection), or a succulent chicken breast featured in a lighter regional specialty, the difference in quality is palpable. Our poultry offers a rich, authentic taste that can only come from birds raised with care and respect, embodying the wholesome and flavourful spirit of Italian home cooking.
