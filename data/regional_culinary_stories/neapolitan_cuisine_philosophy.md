The Vibrant Heart of the South: The Passion of Neapolitan Cuisine

Naples, the bustling capital of Campania, is not only the birthplace of pizza but also a culinary powerhouse whose cuisine is defined by its vibrant, sun-drenched ingredients, robust flavours, and an infectious passion for food. At Ristorante del Cuore, we channel the effervescent spirit of Naples, bringing its authentic tastes and rich traditions to your table.

Defining Characteristics of Neapolitan Cuisine:

The Power of Produce: Campania's fertile volcanic soil, enriched by Mount Vesuvius, yields an abundance of exceptional produce. San Marzano tomatoes, sweet cherry tomatoes, fragrant lemons, and plump mozzarella are the stars of many dishes.

Seafood from the Bay: With the stunning Bay of Naples on its doorstep, fresh seafood is paramount. Anchovies, octopus, clams, mussels, and various white fish are integral to pasta sauces, stews, and simple grilled preparations.

The Reign of Pizza: Neapolitan pizza is more than just food; it's an institution. Its strict rules regarding ingredients and cooking methods create a unique culinary experience, embodying simplicity and perfection.

Pasta and Pulses: Pasta is deeply ingrained, often paired with hearty tomato sauces, seafood, or legumes like beans and lentils, creating wholesome and flavourful meals.

Simple, Bold Flavours: Neapolitan cooking doesn't rely on complex techniques but rather on the inherent quality of its ingredients, allowing their natural, bold flavours to shine through. Garlic, olive oil, basil, and oregano are fundamental.

Sweet Endings: Naples boasts a rich tradition of pastries and desserts, often sweet and decadent, reflecting its historical influences.

Signature Neapolitan Dishes and Inspirations:

Beyond our authentic Neapolitan pizzas, our menu features dishes that capture the essence of Campania:

Spaghetti alle Vongole: A quintessential Neapolitan dish, featuring fresh clams tossed with spaghetti, garlic, chilli, and olive oil – a testament to seafood simplicity.

Pasta e Fagioli: A comforting and hearty pasta and bean soup, a classic example of wholesome cucina povera.

Impepata di Cozze: Steamed mussels with black pepper and lemon, highlighting the freshness of the seafood.

Parmigiana di Melanzane: Layers of fried eggplant, tomato sauce, mozzarella, and basil, baked to perfection.

Our commitment to using authentic San Marzano tomatoes and fior di latte mozzarella permeates many of our dishes, extending beyond just pizza.
