# Bella Terra Menu Offerings Overview

At Bella Terra, we pride ourselves on offering a diverse and authentic Italian culinary experience. Our menu features a wide array of traditional Italian dishes, including:

*   **Authentic Italian Pizzas:** Crafted with fresh, high-quality ingredients and baked to perfection in our traditional oven.
*   **Handmade Pasta Dishes:** A selection of classic and regional pasta recipes, prepared fresh daily.
*   **Exquisite Wines:** A curated list of Italian and international wines to complement every meal.
*   **Craft Beers:** A selection of Italian and local Yorkshire beers.
*   **Delicious Lunch Specials:** A rotating menu of starters, mains, and puddings available during lunch hours.

We are committed to using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients whenever possible, blending the rich flavors of Italy with the best of Yorkshire produce.
