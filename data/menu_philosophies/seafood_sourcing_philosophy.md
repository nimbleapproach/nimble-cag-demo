From Sea to Plate: Our Commitment to Sustainable Seafood

At Ristorante del Cuore, the bounty of the sea holds a special place in our hearts and on our menu. Our seafood philosophy is built upon two unwavering pillars: uncompromising freshness and unwavering commitment to sustainable sourcing. We believe that enjoying the ocean's treasures comes with a responsibility to protect its future.

Our Approach to Freshness:

Daily Catches: We partner directly with trusted fishmongers and select suppliers who prioritize daily landings. This ensures that the seafood reaching our kitchen was often caught just hours before, preserving its delicate flavour and perfect texture.

Local and Italian Waters: While we proudly feature seafood from the British coastline, we also import exquisite varieties directly from the clear waters of the Mediterranean, particularly from the coasts of Sicily and Campania, to ensure authenticity for our regional Italian dishes.

Careful Handling: From boat to kitchen, our seafood is handled with the utmost care to maintain its pristine quality, ensuring it arrives perfectly chilled and ready for preparation.

Our Commitment to Sustainability:

Responsible Fishing Practices: We actively seek out suppliers who adhere to responsible fishing methods that minimize environmental impact and protect marine ecosystems. This includes supporting practices that avoid overfishing and reduce bycatch.

Traceability: Full traceability is paramount. We demand to know the origin of our seafood, allowing us to verify its journey and ensure it meets our ethical and quality standards.

Seasonal Awareness: We are mindful of natural breeding cycles and fishing seasons, adapting our menu to feature species that are abundant and responsibly harvested at different times of the year. This supports marine biodiversity and ensures long-term availability.

Supporting Certified Fisheries: Where possible, we prioritize seafood from fisheries that are certified by reputable organizations for sustainable practices.

This meticulous approach allows us to offer a diverse and exquisite selection of seafood, from the delicate sweetness of our fresh clams in Spaghetti alle Vongole to the robust flavour of our pan-seared swordfish. Every seafood dish at Ristorante del Cuore is a celebration of the ocean's generosity, served with integrity and respect.
