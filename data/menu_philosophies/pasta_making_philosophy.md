The Soul of Italy: Our Artisan Pasta Philosophy

Pasta is the very soul of Italian cuisine, and at Ristorante del Cuore, we honour this tradition by crafting much of our pasta fresh, by hand, every single day. Our pasta-making philosophy is simple yet profound: purity of ingredients, time-honoured techniques, and an unwavering commitment to texture and flavour.

Our journey begins with the finest ingredients:

Durum Wheat Semolina: We exclusively use high-quality semola di grano duro (durum wheat semolina), predominantly sourced from the sun-drenched fields of Puglia, known for producing some of Italy's best wheat. This provides the ideal texture and "bite" for our fresh pasta.

Free-Range Eggs: For our egg-based pastas, we use only the freshest free-range eggs, contributing to a rich colour, tender consistency, and superior flavour.

Pure Water: Simply pure, filtered water completes the trinity of ingredients, allowing the natural flavours of the wheat to shine.

Our Craftsmanship:
Our skilled pastai (pasta makers) blend these simple ingredients with age-old techniques passed down through generations.

Slow Kneading: The dough is slowly kneaded to develop its gluten structure, ensuring elasticity and a perfect al dente texture when cooked.

Traditional Tools: We use traditional rolling pins and pasta machines to achieve the ideal thickness for each specific pasta shape – from the delicate thinness of tagliolini to the hearty robustness of pappardelle.

Regional Diversity: Our menu features a rotating selection of regional pasta shapes, each chosen for its ability to perfectly cradle its accompanying sauce. Expect to find:

Pappardelle: Broad, flat ribbons, ideal for rich, slow-cooked meat ragùs, reminiscent of Tuscan trattorias.

Orecchiette: "Little ears" from Puglia, perfect for capturing vibrant vegetable sauces or simple tomato-based preparations.

Tortellini & Ravioli: Hand-filled with meticulously prepared fillings, reflecting the culinary artistry of Emilia-Romagna.

Spaghetti & Linguine: Our house-made versions elevate these classics, ensuring a superior texture that is rarely found elsewhere.

The result is pasta that is not merely a vehicle for sauce, but a star in its own right – a testament to Italian culinary heritage, made with heart, passion, and an uncompromising dedication to flavour and authenticity. Each strand embodies the warmth and tradition of an Italian home kitchen.
