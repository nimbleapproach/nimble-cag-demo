The Art of the Neapolitan: Our Pizza Philosophy

At Ristorante del Cuore, our pizza is a dedication to the time-honoured traditions of Naples, the birthplace of pizza itself. Our philosophy is rooted in simplicity, superior ingredients, and the magic of the wood-fired oven, delivering an authentic experience that transports you to the bustling pizzerias of Southern Italy.

Our pursuit of pizza perfection is built upon three pillars:

The Dough: A Living Soul

Our pizza dough is the heart of our pizzas. It's crafted daily using just four simple ingredients: highly refined Italian "00" flour, fresh yeast, water, and sea salt.

The dough undergoes a minimum 48-hour slow fermentation process. This extended proofing is crucial, allowing the natural yeast to develop complex flavours and ensuring an incredibly light, airy, and easily digestible crust with a perfect chew. This meticulous process results in the signature slightly crisp exterior and soft, cloud-like interior of true Neapolitan-style pizza.

The Ingredients: Uncompromising Quality

San Marzano Tomatoes: Our tomato sauce is made simply from the finest D.O.P. certified San Marzano tomatoes, grown in the volcanic soil near Mount Vesuvius. Their natural sweetness and vibrant acidity require nothing more than a touch of salt and fresh basil.

Fior di Latte Mozzarella: We use only fresh fior di latte mozzarella, a cow's milk mozzarella known for its delicate flavour and creamy texture that melts beautifully without releasing excessive water.

Premium Toppings: Every additional topping, from our spicy Nduja to our hand-sliced prosciutto, is sourced with the same uncompromising commitment to quality and provenance, celebrating regional Italian specialities and complementing, rather than overpowering, the base ingredients.

The Heat: The Embrace of the Wood-Fired Oven

Our imported, custom-built wood-fired oven is the soul of our kitchen, reaching searing temperatures of over 450°C (900°F).

At this intense heat, our pizzas cook in a mere 60-90 seconds, creating the distinctive cornicione (raised, airy, and slightly charred crust) and ensuring a perfectly cooked, bubbly base with a hint of smoky flavour. The charring, or "leopard spots," on the crust is a hallmark of an authentic Neapolitan pizza, indicative of its rapid bake.

From the classic simplicity of our Margherita to the robust flavours of our Diavola, each pizza at Ristorante del Cuore is a testament to the time, care, and passion that defines Neapolitan pizza-making. It's an experience of true Italian craftsmanship, in every delicious bite.
